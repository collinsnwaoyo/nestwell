# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Collins — the sole real user. Nestwell is a public portfolio prototype that plays the role of the daily tool a Lagos co-living operator would actually run: checking who owes rent, nudging a tenant over WhatsApp, onboarding a new tenant, settling a check-out. It's opened in a focused work session to get something specific done, then closed — not glanced at ambiently, not a marketing surface for outsiders.

## Product Purpose

Nestwell tracks rent, occupancy, and tenancy status across properties, rooms, and beds for a fictional Lagos co-living portfolio, and drives WhatsApp reminders/broadcasts for collection via click-to-chat links (no real messaging API). Success means it reads as software a real operator would trust with their rent roll — not a generic admin-panel template or a consumer fintech app.

## Brand Personality

Structured, utilitarian, confident — spreadsheet-grade clarity over consumer-app softness. Three words: grounded, precise, calm.

## Anti-references

Revolut-style fintech softness: oversized glowing hero numbers, heavy pill/bubble chrome, soft drop-shadow bloom. This was the project's original direction (see CLAUDE.md) and is being actively dropped in favor of something flatter and more Airtable-like. Also avoid generic SaaS-dashboard cliches: gradient-text hero metrics, identical icon+heading filler-card grids, glassmorphism, modal-as-first-reflex.

## Design Principles

- Flat over glowy. Structure, contrast, and hairline borders carry hierarchy — not shadow bloom or soft glow.
- Peers, not a hero metric. Properties, tenants, and revenue are equal-weight facts an operator scans together, not one dominant number with footnotes.
- Real operator data, never decorative. Every figure on screen is an actual Naira amount or a real due date; nothing is there just to look full.
- One confident accent, used sparingly and flatly. A single green carries key actions and status, not a wash across the surface.
- Dense but legible. This is a daily-use operator tool, optimized for scanning, not a marketing surface optimized for first impression.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA contrast is the bar (single real user, demo prototype — no bespoke accessibility requirements beyond that). Tabular figures on every money/numeric column so values align when scanning down a list.
